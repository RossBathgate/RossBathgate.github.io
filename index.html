University of Leeds (Student Projects)

Acoustic Emissions in Rock Cores (2026, Ongoing)
MSc Dissertation analysing high frequency waves emitted during microcrack formation in rock cores under triaxial and uniaxial conditions. Applications include geothermal energy, tunnelling, mining, and earthquake seismology. Utilises a 16-channel ultrasonic detector for source mapping and velocity field analysis, with granite samples used to study stress-history effects.
Cavern Design Project (2026)
Group project applying numerical modelling, joint set analysis, and statistical methods to determine optimal layout and support systems for a deep underground limestone cavern. A room-and-pillar design was selected following comparison with empirical models from literature.
Slope Failure Forensic Report (2025–2026)
Case study of a landslide near the M25 motorway using limit equilibrium methods. Analysis identified a circular slip surface aligned with geological strata as the critical failure mechanism. Mitigation strategies were proposed based on stability calculations.

University of Edinburgh (Student Projects)

Zero-Knowledge Proofs: Removing Interaction from Sigma Protocols
A zero-knowledge proof is a method of proving a statement without revealing anything beyond its validity. This project develops a non-interactive protocol with improved real-world security assumptions, building on prior work. A new transform is implemented in Python and demonstrates improved runtime performance under certain assumptions.
